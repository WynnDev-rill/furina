Mirei visual pass
-----------------
The free VRoid mesh is currently used as a development base. At runtime the
application applies an original Mirei palette inspired by the user's visual
direction: soft pink hair, green/violet eyes, cream clothing, rose accents and
warm gold details. The palette pass is intentionally not presented as a custom
premium sculpt. A future compatible VRM can replace the base without changing
the companion AI, voice, touch or animation state system.

Animation
---------
No third-party motion files are bundled. Body motion, gaze, blinking,
breathing, micro-sway and voice-driven lip movement are generated procedurally
at runtime by src/components/companion/VrmAvatar.tsx.

Optional: drop legally redistributable VRM Animation files into
public/avatar/anim/ and wire them into the existing animation state layer.

Replacing the character
-----------------------
Point VRM_MODEL_URL in src/components/companion/VrmAvatar.tsx at another .vrm
URL. The shared conversation, persona, memory, voice and touch systems do not
need to change.
